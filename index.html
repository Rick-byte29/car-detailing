Products have different jobs. Knowing what each one does makes it easier to choose well — without paying for protection you don't need.

A / PROTECTION

Film or coating?

Paint protection film is a physical layer that can help reduce stone-chip and light surface damage. Ceramic coating adds gloss and water repellency, helping routine cleaning. They are not interchangeable.

Compare protection services +
B / PAINT CARE
✳

Polish or repaint?

Polishing can refine some defects in the clear coat. Deeper scratches and chips may need a paint repair. An inspection helps set realistic expectations before work starts.

Protect the result.

Gentle washing, clean microfibre, and product-specific aftercare help keep a finish looking its best. Follow the care instructions supplied with the selected treatment.

05 / GOOD TO KNOW

Before you
pull in.

PPF or ceramic coating — which do I need?+

PPF is a physical film designed to help reduce stone chips and light surface damage. Ceramic coating helps with gloss, water repellency and easier cleaning. They serve different purposes and can be combined. Neither makes paint damage-proof.

Can every scratch or dent be removed?+

It depends on depth, location and the condition of the paint. Light clear-coat marks may respond to polishing; deeper damage can require repainting. We recommend a treatment after inspection.

How long will my car be at the studio?+

A wash usually takes about 45–90 minutes. Detailed treatments can take a full day, while PPF, coatings and paintwork may need several days including curing. The catalogue gives indicative times.

How should I maintain a coated or wrapped car?+

Follow the installer's curing and maintenance advice. Use suitable pH-neutral wash products and clean microfibre towels, and avoid abrasive brushes. Product-specific care instructions take priority.

Are the displayed prices final?+

These are illustrative starting prices for this demonstration website. Vehicle size, paint condition, product selection, scope and applicable taxes affect the final quote. The estimate builder does not confirm a booking.
